Addendum to the Actronauts design spec

Phone, desktop and browser, updated

These screens show the native iPhone and Android apps (decision D25) and the 22 new desktop and browser abilities (decision D26): what an Actronaut can know about your phone, how it shows up on the Lock Screen and above other apps, what it can do with its hands on each device, and the guards it keeps in the browser.

They reuse the approved spec’s tokens, components and characters unchanged, in both themes and at phone width. Nothing here is built, and every name, number and receipt is an example. Once approved, these screens replace the install flow in part 05 of the spec and join its part 04.

11

Checks

What these screens hold to, from the brand rules and the plan’s security model.

  • Monochrome throughout; amber, red, green and blue appear only for waiting, danger, done and information, always with a text label.
  • No live dots: Mabel’s state is its face, a label or a held border. The bubble is the character itself, not an indicator.
  • Shadows only on things that float: the notification shade, the bubble, popovers and sheets.
  • Nothing is approved from a Lock Screen, a widget, the watch or a notification when it needs a passkey; those open the app.
  • Every sense starts off and shows its last use; every screen read is on request and says it isn’t saved.
  • Browser warnings live in the side panel and the toolbar, which a page can’t draw over.
  • Meeting notes never start without the label and the consent line, and “no notes” stops and deletes them.
  • Sentence case, no dashes as punctuation, Geist at 400, 500 and 600, radii of 4, 6 and 8, and rounded shapes only on characters and people.
  • Both themes, motion off honoured, keyboard reachable controls, and a phone-width layout without sideways scrolling.
12

Design decisions for approval

The choices these screens make that the plan leaves open. Approving the addendum approves these.

N1

One phone app for the whole crew

Personal and Work Actronauts live in one Actronauts app, each with its own widgets, Siri name and alternate icon. The web app keeps one icon per Actronaut for people who don’t install the native app.

N2

Senses are asked for in context

First launch asks only for notifications, places and Focus. Every other sense is offered the first time it would help, at most once a week each, and each row shows its last use.

N3

The Lock Screen never approves

The Dynamic Island, Live Activities, widgets, notifications and the watch show progress and ask, but anything that needs a passkey opens the app for Face ID.

N4

The Android bubble appears with work, not all day

It shows during a mission or while Mabel waits for you, and can be pinned to stay. This is the phone’s version of “on screen by default” (D8), tuned for battery and for small screens.

N5

The keyboard sends nothing until tapped

The draft appears in the keyboard; nothing reaches the app or the cloud until you tap Insert or Mabel’s key, and the keyboard never learns from what you type elsewhere.

N6

Mabel calls you only when it’s urgent

Calls through the app are for urgent approvals and Guardian alerts. Briefs and updates arrive as notifications or texts.

N7

Show what was seen

Select and ask, the Android assistant and the webcam each show a thumbnail or a line saying exactly what Mabel saw, and that it wasn’t saved.

N8

Browser warnings live in the browser’s chrome

The guards speak from the side panel and the toolbar icon, never from an overlay inside the page, so a hostile page can’t fake or hide them.

N9

Receipts say where the work happened

Every receipt is labelled “On this phone”, “On this computer”, “In the cloud” or “Both”, so charter rules such as “health stays on this phone” can be checked.
