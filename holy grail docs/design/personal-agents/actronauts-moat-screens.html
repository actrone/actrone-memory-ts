Addendum to the Actronauts design spec

Rewind, preview, proof and Guardian

These are the screens for the additions in decision D22 that people see and touch: Rewind to undo an hour of work, mission preview before a big task, shareable proof anyone can check, Guardian mode for an older parent, and the early initiative cards.

They reuse the approved spec’s tokens, components and characters unchanged, in both themes and at phone width. Nothing here is built, and every name, number and receipt is an example.

01

Rewind

One place to undo what an Actronaut did. Pick a window of time, untick anything you want to keep, and Rewind reverses the rest: it unsends emails still inside their window, cancels bookings the business still allows, and restores the calendar. Anything that can’t be undone says so, and why, before you start. Phase P2.

02

Mission preview

Before a mission that spends, books or contacts someone new, the Actronaut does a dry run and shows the plan, what it will touch, what it costs and where it will stop to ask. The dry run reads but never writes, so nothing reaches the outside world until you approve. One approval runs the whole mission. Phase P2.

When it appears: any mission with a purchase, a booking, a message to someone new, or more than five steps; small tasks run without it. Dry run: reads booking pages, prices and calendars, and holds every call, message and payment until you approve. Backend: GAL preview plus speculative execution, both already built.

03

Anything an Actronaut did can become a link that someone else can check: a landlord, an employer, an airline or a court. The same page checks a Mandate, which says what an Actronaut may do, for whom and until when, and the code an Actronaut reads out on a call. It proves what happened without showing anything the owner chose to hide. Phase P2; Mandates and calls in P3.

04

Guardian mode

An Actronaut that watches over an older parent, set up with their consent. It screens unknown calls, spots the patterns scammers use, holds payments that break the family’s rules and tells the family. The parent stays in charge: they see every flag, can call anyone back and can pause Guardian any time. It works from what is said and done, never from the tone of anyone’s voice. Phase P3 for call screening, P6 with households.

Consent: Guardian starts only after the parent agrees on their own phone, in their own words, and the agreement is a signed receipt. Control: the parent can pause it for an hour, a day or until they turn it back on, and they always see why something was flagged. Patterns: gift cards, crypto, wire transfers to new payees, remote-access apps, demands for secrecy or urgency, and callers posing as banks, government agencies or relatives. No voice tone or emotion reading (decision D9). Calls: calls from numbers outside the parent’s contacts forward to the Actronaut’s number for screening. Claims: “helps spot scams”, never “prevents fraud”.

06

Decisions for approval

Approving this addendum accepts the choices below. Once approved, these screens fold into the main spec.

R1

Rewind’s default window

The last hour, with 15 minutes and today one tap away. Everything that can still be undone starts ticked.

R2

How Rewind reports

Newest first, one signed reversal receipt per action, and partial results reported row by row with what to do next.

P1

When a mission gets a preview

Purchases, bookings, messages to someone new, or more than five steps. Everything else runs without one.

P2

What the dry run may do

Read only. If the chosen option changes between the preview and the real run, the Actronaut asks again.

V1

What a shared proof shows

Initials by default; the full name only if the owner chooses. Links last 7 days by default and can be revoked any time.

V2

Who can check a proof

Anyone with the link, with no account and no cookies. The signature is checked in the browser as well as on the server.

G1

Guardian consent

The parent agrees on their own phone, can pause Guardian any time, and is always told why something was flagged.

G2

Held payments

One named family member approves with a passkey. The parent can always call them first, and nobody else sees the parent’s accounts.

I1

The nudge budget

Three a day by default, each with its source; urgent items ignore the budget, and dismissals teach the Actronaut to ask less.
