## 0. The job of hosted marketing

Where OSS marketing manufactures *installs*, hosted marketing manufactures **provable trust and
qualified demand** for the governance buyer (platform / security / compliance / eng-leadership). It
launches into the warm OSS base each region already has (that's why OSS leads every geo). Its job:
**make "governance-first for production AI agents" a category the market recognizes, and make Actrone
the proven answer — with public evidence, not adjectives.**

**Honest constraints it must respect (deep code sweep):**
- **Sell the shipped governance spine** (MAL / DPE / audit / trust / Governed Action Layer) — that's
  load-bearing and demoable. **Position the flywheels/cost-savings and voice-on-live-calls as "built,
  proving out," never "delivering today"** (the flywheels idle until traffic + keys; voice is deploy-
  gated). **"SOC 2 Type II in progress," not achieved.**
- **Standardize the category noun first:** *"the governed control plane for AI agents — self-hosted, any model."* Fix the homepage (currently "governed runtime") to match before spend.

## 1. Positioning (the hosted front door)

> **The governed control plane for AI agents — self-hosted, any model.**
> *Everyone else helps you build, connect, or route agents. Actrone is where they run — every action
> clears deterministic policy and a signed audit trail before it executes.*

**The thesis (consistent across all buyer content):** *accountability, not capability.* We don't sell
another way to build agents; we sell the thing that lets you **put them in production on the systems
that matter** — because no CFO/CISO/regulator grants an autonomous agent access to a system of record
without proof, reversibility, and hard bounds. **"They make agents possible; Actrone makes them
deployable."**

**The empty-cell claim (the sharpest line, and it's true):** across workflow/iPaaS, unified-API, voice
AI, agent frameworks, and LLM gateways — *not one governs the agent's action on an enterprise system,
field-by-field, end-to-end. That empty cell is the moat.*

## 2. The buyer + the buying committee

| Persona | What they fear | What wins them |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Platform / eng lead** (champion) | agents that work in demo, fail/leak in prod | the runtime + audit trail + "run any framework governed" |
| **Security / CISO** | data egress, un-auditable actions, blast radius | zero-egress, tamper-evident audit, simulate-then-commit, the pen-test + trust center |
| **Compliance / DPO / risk** | GDPR/HIPAA/FSCA exposure, regulator questions | residency, per-tenant DPA/BAA, signed regulator exports |
| **Economic buyer** (VP Eng / CTO) | vendor sprawl, cost drift, procurement risk | "replace five vendors, pass the security review," the savings-dividend |

Governance only *lands* when the security/compliance stakeholder is in the room — hosted marketing's
job is to arm them with self-serve proof *before* sales ever calls.

## 3. The 10x plays

1. **"Show, don't claim" — the 5 proof artifacts are the marketing** (GTM §3): the audit-trail demo,
   the **interpreter demo** (the launch hero — demos in 60s, no traffic needed), the savings-dividend
   invoice, the zero-egress reference architecture, and the public trust center + pen-test report.
   These *are* the campaign; feature depth is the closing tool, not the opener.
2. **Category creation, owned vocabulary:** *AgentOps · governed action layer · zero-egress agents ·
   production AI agents.* Publish the canonical thesis and the "governance checklist for production
   agents" (a scored lead magnet that doubles as a security-review artifact).
3. **The savings-dividend as a PR weapon:** *"we only bill the margin against savings we can prove —
   capped at measured savings, excess auto-credited."* It's actually coded (`billing/reconcile.go`).
   Once one real invoice exists, it's the least-copyable claim in the category.
4. **Radical honesty as enterprise trust:** publish the "honest gaps," the "demo only what survives
   production" ethos, the pen-test *including findings*. In a category of overclaim, candor is
   differentiating — and it's already the brand's voice.
5. **The interpreter as the wedge into voice/regulated buyers:** PII-safe, glossary-locked, brand-voice
   live translation that *no competitor offers* — a single demo that opens fintech/health/gov/BPO doors.
6. **Region-tailored proof (see §5):** same platform, different lead artifact per geo. Sovereignty for
   EU/Africa; cost + accountability for US/Canada.

## 4. Channels (buyer-led, month 5+)

- **Category content + SEO** for the governance buyer: the pillars (production failures, the checklist,
  zero-egress, AgentOps thesis) — ranking on the buyer's search, arming the security review.
- **Webinars + live demos:** "pass your AI security review" walkthroughs, the interpreter demo, the
  audit-trail deep-dive. Recorded → gated → sales enablement.
- **Account-based marketing (ABM)** into the lighthouse ICP + look-alikes (pairs with
  [Sales — Hosted](./Actrone_Sales_Strategy_Hosted.md) and the [Lighthouse Playbook](./Actrone_Lighthouse_Outreach_Playbook.md)).
- **Trust-center-led inbound:** the public trust center + SECURITY.md + pen-test as a self-serve
  security-review shortcut — governance buyers screenshot these into their own reviews.
- **Analyst + community credibility:** brief the analysts covering AgentOps/LLM-governance; earn
  mentions in security + platform-eng communities (peer trust > ads for this buyer).
- **Marketplace + procurement rails (late W1):** AWS Marketplace private offers to collapse
  procurement; co-sell where possible.
- **Field/events (selective):** regulated-vertical + platform-eng conferences in the launch geos.

**Do NOT lead with paid ads.** Governance buyers trust proof + peers, not banners. Paid *amplifies*
proof after it exists (see [Ad Campaign](./Actrone_Ad_Campaign.md)).

## 7. Guardrails

- **One category noun** (control plane) everywhere; fix the homepage first.
- **"Built, proving out"** for flywheels/cost-savings; **demo, don't promise** voice-on-live-calls;
  **"SOC 2 in progress"**; **data-law frameworks = "mapped + enforced," never "certified/compliant"**
  until counsel clears the jurisdiction. Never outrun the code.
- **Lead with one provable moat, not breadth** (the interpreter / audit trail). Breadth closes; it
  doesn't open.
- **On-brand always:** monochrome Black & Apple-Silver, Geist, sentence case, signature effects, no
  gradients — the restraint is the differentiator in a gradient-soaked category.
