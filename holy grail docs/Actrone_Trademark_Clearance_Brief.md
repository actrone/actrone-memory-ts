## 1. The mark & where to file

- **Mark:** `ACTRONE`, a standard-character word mark, used as a **product / brand name** (protect the name in any
  font/case first; a logo/stylised mark can follow separately). *Trademark law is indifferent to whether the mark
  is a company name or a product name; it protects the brand **as used on the goods/services** either way.*
- **Applicant / owner:** the **operating company that makes Actrone**, which may carry a **different legal name**.
  That company files for and owns the ACTRONE mark; you do **not** need the company to be named "Actrone" to hold
  the mark. (If an IP holdco owns the mark while a separate entity ships the product, put a **quality-controlled
  licence** in place so the registration stays valid.)
- **Jurisdictions (priority order):**
  1. **US: USPTO** (1(b) intent-to-use to lock priority now, or 1(a) if already in use; see §6).
  2. **EU: EUIPO** (one filing covers all 27 member states).
  3. **UK: UKIPO** (separate post-Brexit; not covered by EUIPO).
  4. **International: WIPO Madrid Protocol** (based on the US or EU home application; cost-efficient for adding
     further countries later).
- **Paris Convention priority:** file the remaining jurisdictions **within 6 months** of the first filing to
  back-date their priority to the first filing date.

## 2. Classes & drafted goods/services (Nice Classification)

File in **Class 9 + Class 42** (the standard pairing for a SaaS platform that also ships downloadable
SDKs/libraries). **The descriptions are deliberately drafted to sit far from ACTRON's automotive-diagnostics
goods** (no "vehicle", "automotive", "diagnostic", "scan tool" language) to strengthen a coexistence argument.

**Class 9, downloadable and recorded software:**
> Downloadable computer software for building, orchestrating, governing, and monitoring artificial-intelligence
> agents; downloadable software development kits (SDKs) and application programming interface (API) software;
> downloadable software libraries for artificial-intelligence memory, context management, and data governance;
> downloadable computer software for policy enforcement, access control, and audit logging of autonomous software
> agents.

**Class 42, SaaS, PaaS and software development:**
> Software as a service (SaaS) featuring software for building, orchestrating, governing, and monitoring
> artificial-intelligence agents; platform as a service (PaaS) featuring computer-software platforms for autonomous
> AI-agent execution, governance, and observability; providing temporary use of non-downloadable software for
> AI-agent workflow orchestration, cost management, and policy enforcement; design and development of computer
> software; cloud-computing services featuring software for AI-agent governance and data security.

**Optional (only if the business warrants it):**
- **Class 35**: if the marketplace becomes a storefront for third-party goods/services (online-marketplace/
  advertising services). Not required for the core platform; adds cost.
- **Class 42 already covers** the marketplace *software*; Class 35 is only for the *commercial* marketplace act.

## 6. Risk read & coexistence argument (for the attorney to confirm)

Likelihood-of-confusion factors (US *DuPont* / equivalent elsewhere):

| Factor | Assessment for ACTRONE vs ACTRON | Direction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Similarity of marks (sight/sound/meaning) | Very close; they differ by a silent trailing 'e' | ✗ against us |
| **Relatedness of goods/services** | AI-agent governance SaaS vs automotive diagnostic tools/hardware: **distant** | ✓✓ **our strongest factor** |
| Trade channels / buyers | Enterprise dev/AI buyers vs auto technicians: different | ✓ for us |
| Buyer sophistication | Enterprise software buyers are sophisticated → less confusion | ✓ for us |
| Strength / resources of senior mark | ACTRON is established with a well-resourced owner (Bosch) that may police | ✗ against us |

**Net:** coexistence looks **plausible** (the industries barely touch), but the exposure concentrates in the
**Class 9 software overlap** and Bosch's willingness to police. Mitigations: (a) keep the Class 9 description
**tightly AI-specific** (as drafted in §2), (b) be ready to argue distinct channels/buyers, (c) the attorney may
advise a narrowed description or, if needed, a coexistence/consent approach. **"Exact name is free" is necessary
but not sufficient; the ACTRON-family opinion is the real deliverable.**
